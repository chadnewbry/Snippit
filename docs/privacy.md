# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 12, 2026

**Chad Newbry, LLC** ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Snippit mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Collage Data
- **Collage projects** — images, clippings, stickers, and text you add to your collages
- **Magazine clippings** — content you clip from the integrated magazine browser

All collage data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData.

## Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal account data** — the App does not require account creation or collect your name, email, or profile information
- **Location data** — we do not request or store your location
- **Analytics or tracking** — we do not use third-party analytics or ad tracking SDKs
- **Photos are not uploaded** — your images stay on your device and are never sent to external servers

## How We Use Your Information
- To provide the App's core collage creation features
- To process on-device AI smart clipping using Apple's Vision framework (all processing happens locally on your device — no data is sent to external servers)

## Third-Party Services

Snippit does not use any third-party services that process your personal data. All functionality runs entirely on your device.

## Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

- **Photo Library** — to import photos into your collages

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device Settings.

## Data Storage & Security

All your data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. We do not operate any cloud servers or databases. Your collages and content never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
