## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create a collage?
Tap the "+" button to start a new project. Choose a canvas size, then add clippings from the magazine browser, photos from your library, stickers, text, and decorations.

### How does smart clipping work?
Snippit uses on-device AI (Apple's Vision framework) to intelligently extract subjects from images. Simply select an image and tap the smart clip button — the AI will detect and isolate the main subject for you. All processing happens on your device.

### How accurate is smart clipping?
Smart clipping works well for most images with clear subjects. Results may vary for complex backgrounds or overlapping subjects. You can always manually refine clippings after the AI extraction.

### Is my data private?
Yes. All your collages and data are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to external servers. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy) for full details.

### How do I export or share my collages?
Use the share button within a collage project to export as an image or share directly to social media and messaging apps.

### Can I recover a deleted collage?
Once a collage is deleted, it cannot be recovered. We recommend exporting important collages before deleting them.
